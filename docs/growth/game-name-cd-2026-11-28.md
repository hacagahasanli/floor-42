## I think it's PEWFIELD.

Backups, in order: **Duelfield**, then **Grudge Games**.

## 0. Problems and risks first

- **Pewfield does not say "1v1" on its own.** It says "shooting" (pew pew) and "sports field". The portal title and the thumbnail have to carry the 1v1 part: CG title `Pewfield: 2 Player Duel` [suggestion for growth to test], with two fighters facing each other on every cover. If Reis wants the name itself to say 1v1, take Duelfield.
- **"Pew" has three other meanings.** A church bench; "pew!" (US kids' word for a bad smell); the Pew Research Center / Pew Charitable Trusts. None is a game. The smell joke fits a goofy game; the other two are far away in meaning. [Assumption: no player tested.]
- **The "PEW PEW" trademark family exists.** PEWPEW (US reg. 4893686, live, class 13 firearms and parts, an individual owner) and PEW PEW PENGUIN (serial 86532387, computer game programs, status not seen). Pewfield is one coined word with a different overall look and sound, so the risk is low, not zero. [Assumption: not legal advice.]
- **"Pew Pew" game titles exist** on CrazyGames (`pew-pew-cec`, a solo arcade shooter) and itch.io (several, one with local 2P on one keyboard). A search for "pew pew" will not find us; a search for "pewfield" should.
- **Trademark checks are mirrors, not clearance.** Justia returned HTTP 403 to automated requests, so I used web search over Justia/markinton result snippets. USPTO, EUIPO and WIPO were not queried (growth found the same blocks). Poki could not be reached at all from here (`poki.com/en/g/<slug>` and the known-good `subway-surfers` both timed out, HTTP 000), so Poki was checked only by site-limited web search.
- **No player has heard the name.** Cheapest test: show the thumbs PNG to 5-10 kids/teens, cover the names after 3 seconds, ask them to write down the name they saw. Whichever name they spell right most often wins ties.

## 1. Why Pewfield wins

| Test | Pewfield |
|------|----------|
| Thumbnail read | One word, 8 letters, no descenders, wide round letters in Titan One. At 200x113 it still reads (see PNG). The logo has a ready-made icon: the "PEW" burst reads at 48 px. |
| Sound | Two beats, "PYOO-feeld". The first beat is the sound the game makes. Nothing to mishear. |
| Memorability | It is a sound effect plus a place. Kids already say "pew pew" when they mime a gun, in many languages, because of cartoons and memes. [Assumption from common usage, not measured.] |
| Fits the world | Pewfield becomes the company town: the offsite field day is held there and the office is on Floor 42 of the Pewfield building. The OFFSITE water tower can say PEWFIELD, the way real towns paint their name on the tower. Works for both arenas, all four fighters, no developer joke. |
| Works across languages | Only two common English parts. [Assumption: I know of no rude meaning in Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Turkish, Russian or Azerbaijani; not checked with native speakers, see section 4.] |
| Spelling | Spelled as it sounds. Only likely typo: "pew field" (two words): redirect it. |
| Ownable | Zero exact hits on every store and portal checked, zero web hits, no mark found, pewfield.com unregistered. |
| Kid-safe | A cartoon sound, not a weapon word. No "kill", "gun", "brawl". |

Why the backups lose: **Duelfield** says 1v1 best, but spoken it is almost "dual wield" (itch search for "duelfield" returns "DualWield" first), people will type "dualfield", and it sits next to Konami's DUEL marks and the growth's Duel Day. **Grudge Games** tells the story best ("Two coworkers. One grudge.") and "GG" is nice, but "grudge" is a hard word for young non-native players, Google Play's top hit for it is the horror app "The Grudge", and grudgegames.com is taken.
