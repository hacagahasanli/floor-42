# Game name for "Floor 42" (R-023 input), 2026-11-28

Owner: game-search-growth. Status: recommendation; Reis decides. Nothing registered or submitted.

Context: the developer theme is being removed. The new default arena is the bright desert canyon OFFSITE (a company field day), the UI is "Field Day", and the office stays as a second map. The game is a side-on 1v1 shooter/brawler: 2 players on one keyboard or vs a bot, desktop, CrazyGames first. It is not on CrazyGames yet (RELEASE-QA-crazygames-2026-11-27: submittable after B1-B3), so a rename now costs almost nothing in search equity. After launch it would cost a CG re-review and split the entity.

## 0. Problems and risks first

- **Every short "fight word + noun" name is crowded.** No candidate is fully ownable. "Duel", "Brawl", "Clash", "Showdown" and "Rumble" each have dozens of game titles and trademarks. Being unique comes from the pair ("Duel Day"), not from either word.
- **Supercell owns BRAWL STARS (US reg. 5567901) and BRAWL ESPORTS (US reg. 6564853, filed 2021-02-12, class 9).** It also owns the CLASH brands. A Supercell application for bare BRAWL (serial 79166935, 2015) is abandoned. Source: Justia mirrors (links in section 3), not USPTO TSDR. Any "Brawl" or "Clash" name carries a small risk of a complaint from a company that enforces its marks. [Assumption: enforcement intensity not measured.]
- **"Duel" is often misspelled "dual".** Kids and non-native players will type "dual day". This is cheap to fix with a redirect subdomain (section 5).
- **Trademark checks here are not clearances.** USPTO tmsearch.uspto.gov and EUIPO eSearch are script apps. Automated requests failed: the USPTO API returned MethodNotAllowed, and EUIPO gave no response (HTTP 000). WIPO Global Brand DB loaded (HTTP 200) but needs an interactive session. I used public mirrors (trademarks.justia.com, markinton.com, trademarkdir.com) through web search. "No hit" means no hit in those mirrors, nothing more.
- **Poki was not queried directly.** poki.com/en/g/<slug> timed out from here, so Poki was checked by site-limited web search only.
- **Search volumes are not measured.** I had no keyword tool, so every competition and intent note below is qualitative. [Estimate] labels apply.

## 3. SEO and subdomain notes

Players do not search a new game's name; they search the category: "2 player games", "2 player shooting games", "fighting games", "duel games". [Estimate: these are high-volume, high-competition head terms owned by the portals' category pages.] Our page wins on the long tail ("2 player shooter same keyboard", "1v1 gun game browser") and on branded queries once people know the name. So the name has two jobs: (a) be unambiguous when typed, so a branded query returns us; (b) carry a category word for the thumbnail and the portal title.

| Name | Category word in name | Branded SERP noise [Estimate] | Typing risk | Subdomain |
|------|----------------------|-------------------------------|-------------|-----------|
| Duel Day | "duel" = 1v1, matches portal titles ("Soccer Duel", "Escape School Duel") | low; exact phrase has no game | "dual day" | duelday.nalbakigames.com (7 chars) + redirect dualday |
| Brawl Day | "brawl" = fight | medium; Brawl Stars dominates every "brawl" query | low | brawlday.nalbakigames.com |
| KO Day | "KO" = knockout | high; "ko" is a language code and a common token | "k.o. day", "kday" | koday.nalbakigames.com |
| Rumble Day | weak for non-native players | high; Rumble (video platform), Rumble Club | low | rumbleday... (10) |
| Showdown Day | medium | medium; Hunt: Showdown, Brawl Stars mode | long | showdownday... (12, clunky) |
| Offsite Brawl | "brawl" | lowest; unique pair | "off site" | offsitebrawl... (12) |

Domains: duelday.com was registered in 2020 and did not resolve on 2026-11-28; brawlday.com is unregistered (whois "No match"); koday.com was registered in 1996. We only need the subdomain, so this is information only.

Subdomain DNS: duelday, brawlday and koday under nalbakigames.com do not resolve yet. floor42.nalbakigames.com is live on Vercel. Same pattern as the Hazefall rename: a new subdomain, then a 301 from floor42.nalbakigames.com/* to the new host, path for path.

## 4. Ranked top 3

1. **Duel Day.**
   - Says 1v1 at a glance. Two short words that kids worldwide know from portal titles.
   - "Day" echoes the Field Day UI and the offsite.
   - A duel in a desert canyon is a ready-made Western image for the cover.
   - Cleanest checks: no game with this exact title on CG, Poki, Steam, the app stores or itch, and no DUEL DAY mark in the mirrors.
   - Risks: "dual" typos (fix with a redirect); "duel" is a common word, so the name cannot be owned alone; Konami's DUEL-formative card-game marks [low risk: different words, different genre].
2. **Brawl Day.**
   - The most "fun fight" feel for kids.
   - Clean exact-name checks; the .com is unregistered.
   - Risks: sits in Supercell's BRAWL space (live BRAWL STARS and BRAWL ESPORTS marks) and Brawl Stars buries every "brawl" search. Branded discovery is weaker and the complaint risk is higher than Duel Day's.
3. **KO Day.**
   - Shortest name, biggest letters on a thumbnail.
   - Risks: a live bare KO mark in classes 9/41 (Fanspoko); "ko" makes a noisy branded SERP; younger kids may not read "KO" as "knockout".

Not recommended: Offsite Brawl (most ownable, but "offsite" is office jargon that kids and non-native players miss, and it names the wrong map when the office is picked); Rumble Day and Showdown Day (weak or long, crowded SERPs).

Keep "FLOOR 42" as the office map's name (and OFFSITE as the canyon's). That keeps the old name visible in the game so early players and links still connect, at no cost.
